How do I get ClassSeats Companion on my phone?

Set up your classes, rosters, and seating charts on a desktop or laptop first. Then install ClassSeats Companion on your phone for quick classroom access.

Start on your computer

  1. Open ClassSeats on your desktop or laptop.
  2. Set up your classes, student rosters, and seating charts.

Install ClassSeats Companion

Choose the store for your phone. These links open the official ClassSeats Companion app listings.

Open the app on your phone and follow its setup prompts.

For current app information, see the ClassSeats Companion page.

Remove the old Home Screen bookmark

If you previously added mobile.classseats.app to your Home Screen, press and hold that icon and remove the old bookmark. Use the Companion app instead.
